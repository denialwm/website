Denial Mobile

A Linux phone you’d actually use.

Denial Mobile is the Denial compositor as a touch-first shell for phones and tablets. Gestures, fingerprint unlock, mobile data, and your Android apps, on Linux.

Get Denial Mobile See devices

These are Linux mobile devices! Not Android, and not a PC.

The shell

Built for touch, from the compositor up.

The mobile shell has its own home screen, recents, quick settings, notifications and keyboard, all drawn by Denial itself.

  • Home

    A paged app grid with your wallpaper.

  • Recents

    Every open app, one swipe away.

  • Notifications

    One history for Linux and Android apps alike.

  • Keyboard

    Built into the compositor, for every app, Android ones included.

  • Lock screen

    A session lock with fingerprint and password unlock.

  • Quick settings

    Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data, brightness, rotation and desktop view.

The hardware

Fingerprint, SIM, rotation and haptics.

Fingerprint unlock

Under-display sensors work through Denial’s own Wayland protocols. Verification starts the moment the session locks, and your password always works too.

Mobile data and SIM

Carrier, signal and data status from ModemManager. Toggle mobile data from quick settings and unlock your SIM with its PIN.

Auto-rotation

The shell and your apps follow the device through iio-sensor-proxy, with a rotation lock in quick settings.

Haptics

Tap feedback and a buzz on a rejected fingerprint, through hapticd.

Denial uses the system services already on the device. Each feature shows up when its hardware and service are there.

Convergence

Same Denial, any screen.

Plug any Denial phone or tablet into a monitor and it becomes the desktop: stacking, tiling and scrolling layouts, the full shell, and the same apps you were just using with your fingers.

Android apps

Your apps come with you.

Moving to a Linux phone usually means leaving your apps behind, but with Droidloom they come along. It runs Android apps natively on ARM, each in its own window: Google Play, your messengers, even demanding games.

  • Laid out like on Android

    Apps see Denial’s status bar and gesture bar as their own and lay out around them.

  • Denial’s keyboard

    Text fields in Android apps open Denial’s on-screen keyboard.

  • Notifications

    Android notifications land in Denial’s notification history.
