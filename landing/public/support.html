      What you’re funding

      Built from scratch, in the open.

      Denial

      A Wayland compositor written in Flutter. One codebase for the desktop, the tablet and the phone, with a shell you can edit live while it runs.

      See Denial →

      Droidloom

      Real Android apps as real Linux windows. No VM, zero-copy graphics, and its own protocols so apps feel at home on Denial.

      See Droidloom →

      Next goal

      A new device on the desk.

      Every port starts with the hardware in hand. Donations toward this goal buy the next device to bring Denial to, and the port that comes out of it is free for everyone, like everything else.

      Device ports

      Want Denial on your device?

      Porting a specific phone or tablet for you is paid work, agreed case by case and separate from sponsoring. The Denial Mobile page lists what a device needs and how to ask.
